PINGLISH - PRIVACY

Pinglish has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads.

STAYS ON YOUR PC
- Everything you type and its conversion to Persian (done entirely on your PC).
- Your spelling corrections, settings and the start-up log, in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pinglish.

SENT ONLY WHEN YOU USE THE MICROPHONE
When you press the mic button, Pinglish sends your recorded speech over HTTPS
to Google for speech recognition, phrase by phrase, until you stop it.
Nothing is recorded or sent while the mic is off.
- By default: Google's web speech service (the one Chrome uses).
- With your own Google Cloud key: Google Cloud Speech-to-Text in your project.
Google processes this audio under the Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
Pinglish itself does not store your audio.

TURNING IT OFF
On the next page you can untick "Enable speech recognition". The mic button
then does nothing and no audio is ever sent. Run this installer again to
change the choice.

Full policy: https://github.com/MindDriverTUWIEN/Pinglish/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
